# Code of Conduct

JTalk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Contributors must help maintain an open, welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the project, and avoid discriminatory,
threatening, sexualized, or personally identifying content.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when conduct is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository's security-reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as practical.
